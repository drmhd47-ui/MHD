Privacy policy

Version: 2026-09-29

Data controller

M GROUP (M GRP Company For Lawyership and Legal Consultations), a licensed professional law firm and limited liability company (professional), Commercial Registration 7055255900, Law Practice Licence 481757, located at King Abdullah Financial District (KAFD), Riyadh 13519. For anything relating to your data: info@mgrp.sa or +966 546 444 000.

This policy is issued in compliance with the Personal Data Protection Law and its implementing regulations, and with the duty of professional confidentiality under the Law of Practice of Law.

Data we collect

Only when you submit the consultation request form, we collect:

  • Your full name or organisation name.
  • Your preferred contact method, mobile number and/or email address.
  • The service requested (optional).
  • The name of the other party in the matter (optional), used only to check for conflicts of interest.
  • A record of your consent: its time, the version of this policy you accepted and the site language.

The form does not ask for documents or details about your matter, and we ask you not to include them.

When you browse without submitting the form: we use no cookies, no analytics, tracking or advertising tools, and we load no fonts or other resources from third-party servers. The web server keeps technical logs (IP address, time and page requested) for security and troubleshooting, deleted automatically after 30 days.

Purposes and legal basis

PurposeLegal basis
Responding to and following up on your requestYour consent, and processing necessary to take steps at your request before a contract
Checking for conflicts of interest before accepting the requestThe lawyer’s professional obligation
Protecting the site from abuse (rate limiting)Legitimate interest in service security, without storing your IP address with the request

We do not use your data for marketing, sell it, or share it for advertising.

Who can see your data

  • Authorised members of the firm who review incoming requests, through an internal system protected by two-factor authentication, role-based permissions and an audit log.
  • Technical service providers hosting the website and the intake server and sending the automatic acknowledgement, under contractual obligations of data protection and confidentiality.
  • Competent authorities where required by law.

Where your data is stored

The website, the intake server and the internal system are hosted inside the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia, and we do not transfer your data outside it.

If you choose to contact us via WhatsApp, that conversation is subject to WhatsApp’s terms and privacy policy and may be processed outside the Kingdom. We therefore recommend not sending documents or confidential details through it before speaking with a lawyer.

Retention

  • Intake server: keeps an encrypted copy of the request until it is delivered to the internal system, then deletes it immediately. If delivery fails, it is deleted after 7 days at most.
  • Requests that do not become an engagement: personal data is deleted or anonymised 90 days after the request is closed.
  • Requests that become an engagement: become part of the client file and are kept for the retention periods applicable to professional files.

Your rights

Under the Personal Data Protection Law you have the right to:

  • Be informed of how and on what basis your data is collected and processed.
  • Access your data and obtain a copy.
  • Request correction, completion or update of your data.
  • Request destruction of your data when no longer needed, unless there is a legal ground to retain it.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, email info@mgrp.sa and we will respond within the period set by law. You may also lodge a complaint with the Saudi Data & AI Authority (SDAIA).

Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS) on every page, encryption of requests stored temporarily on the intake server, digital signatures on messages between the intake server and the internal system, and two-factor authentication and a tamper-resistant audit log in the internal system. Internal notification messages contain no personal data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our processing or legal requirements change; the version date appears at the top of this page. Each request records the policy version its sender accepted.
